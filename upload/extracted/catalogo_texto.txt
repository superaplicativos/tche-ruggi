BIOGRAFIA
“I AM FORMED BY THE WHOLE OF MY EXPERIENCES”.
HIS WORKS TRANSCEND THE LIMITS OF TRADITIONAL GRAFFITI AND BLEND THE
CONTEMPORARY WITH THE GEOMETRIC ABSTRACT. HIS CREATION COMES FROM
EXPERIENCE, WHETHER IN THE URBAN ENVIRONMENT OR IN NATURE, THE LINES
OF THE CITY OR LIVING WITH ROCKS AND CRYSTALS AND FREQUENT VISITS TO
MINES AND THE UNIVERSE OF PRECIOUS STONES, IN CHILDHOOD, TRANSFORMED
NATURAL FORMS INTO A SYMBOL OF MEMORY, CONTRIBUTING TO THE CREATION
OF THE ARTIST'S UNIVERSE IN HIS WORKS.
MARCELO RUGGI. ALSO KNOWN AS TCHÉ, IS A BRAZILIAN URBAN ARTIST,
MURALIST AND SCULPTOR, BORN IN SÃO PAULO, LIVED HIS CHILDHOOD IN BELO
HORIZONTE AND STARTED GRAFFITI IN THE 2000S ON THE COAST OF PAULO. IN
2008, IN SÃO PAULO, HE BROUGHT TOGETHER ARTISTS AND OPENED THE ART
STUDIO, COLETIVO132 AND IN 2012, WITH PARTNERS, HE OPENED THE ART AND
CULTURE SPACE (ASSÉTIMA) A7MA GALERIA, LOCATED IN BECO DO BATMAN,
OPENING UP SPACE FOR EMERGING ARTISTS AND CONSOLIDATING CAREERS.
IN SHORT, TCHÉ IS AN ARTIST WHO SEEKS TO MAKE HIS CREATIONS REVERBERATE
BETWEEN LINES AND SHAPES, SPARKLES AND SHADOWS, STRUCTURES AND ALLEYS,
RECREATING GEOMETRIC NATURAL ENVIRONMENTS, WITH WORKS THAT ECHO THE
ESSENCE OF THE CITY, OF LIFE AND OF NATURE, OPENING OUR EYES TO THE
IMPORTANCE OF HAVING THIS IN BALANCE.
HIS STYLE IS A FUSION OF THE URBAN, NATURE AND THE PERSONAL, CHALLENGING
CONVENTIONS AND EXPLORING THE TRIAD OF EXISTENCE IN HARMONY. HIS WORKS
BEAR CHARACTERISTICS OF TRANSGRESSION, INFLUENCED BY GRAFFITI AND
PIXAÇÃO FROM SÃO PAULO.

PAINTINGS SERIES:
ENIGMA - 2018
Exhibition held in 2020 at
Galeria A7MA - SP, Brazil.
São Paulo
The ENIGMA exhibition, presented in 2020, is about a
dive into the artist's universe, exploring natural
environments, where the artist recreated a cave full of
rocks and crystals, multifaceted by geometric fractals.
There was a carpet of synthetic grass at the entrance,
with a scent in the air, the soundtrack in the space and
the precious stones in the form of works, positioned on
the walls.
